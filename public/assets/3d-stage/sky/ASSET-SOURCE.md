- Runtime preparation: the official tonemapped panorama was resized to
  3072×1536 (desktop) and 1536×768 (mobile), then exported as progressive JPEG.

The original 8K-class JPEG is retained under `source-assets/3d-stage/sky/`.
The two runtime variants keep the deployed sky payload small while preserving
the equirectangular 2:1 projection required by Three.js.
